## Capture and edit guardrails

- Use existing, retained evidence and authored explanatory graphics only. No filming-time model call, product run, test run, or recreated “live” run.
- Never intercut Scene 3 with Scene 1 footage in a way that suggests a crash or recovery occurred during the successful Live Proof.
- Keep the Scene 2 corrupt-artifact refusal visibly separate from the canonical successful task. It is a retained deterministic example, not a new negative run.
- Any comparison graphic is descriptive and neutral: it contrasts where responsibilities are shown, not measured outcomes or capability superiority.
- Do not show unsupported provider-reported model identity, production reliability, arbitrary exactly-once behavior, universal model support, or OS-wide filesystem conclusions.
- Show the SHA-256 only as the known artifact digest from baseline §7; do not suggest it identifies a new capture.
- Final takeaway: **“AI 说完成，不等于任务真的完成。”** Follow with “模型提出；运行时决定；Verifier 验收。”
